VNPS FLASH PHOTOGRAPHY LEVEL 1 — CLASS CHECKLIST

CAMERA
- Manual exposure mode
- RAW file format
- ISO 100–400
- Aperture f/4–f/8
- Shutter speed 1/60–1/200 s
- Confirm maximum normal sync speed

WIRELESS
- Transmitter seated and locked
- Same channel on transmitter and all remote flashes
- Group A = Key
- Group B = Fill
- Group C = Background or Rim
- Test-fire before posing the subject

ONE-LIGHT PORTRAIT START
- Camera: ISO 100, f/5.6, 1/160 s
- Flash: Manual 1/16 or 1/8
- Channel 1, Group A
- Light 45 degrees to one side
- Modifier above eye level and close to subject
- Subject 6–8 feet from background

FINAL CHECK
- Ambient brightness intentional
- Subject brightness intentional
- Catchlights clean
- Highlights protected
- Background edges clean
- Expression and pose refined
